How it works

  1. Story: a short funny gully-cricket story is picked from the story bank. A free AI model writes 2 new stories a day, and each one must pass the same quality rules as a hand-written story.
  2. Voice: every character speaks in Hindi with a text-to-speech voice (Kokoro).
  3. Animation: the cartoon scenes are rendered into a vertical 25 second video with subtitles.
  4. Quality check: length, subtitles inside the safe area, one end card, spelling, hook and brand rules are all checked. A reel that fails is never queued.
  5. End card: the DASHX logo, a product pack and the call to action to search for the ball on Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho.
  6. Queue: a reel that passes is added to the queue for the next free slot.
  7. Publish: YouTube Shorts are uploaded ahead of time and made public at the slot. Instagram and Facebook Reels are posted at the slot.
  8. First comment: the buy links are added as the first comment after the reel goes live.
