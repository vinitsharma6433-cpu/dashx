# Story bank (fallback when Gemma is not ready or cannot write a valid story)

Hand-written gali-cricket stories for `npm run daily`. Used top to bottom; a story whose title is already in
`stories/used.md` is skipped. Every ending is different.

Format: `## <English title> | <Hindi title>`, then `plot:`, `ending:` (3 to 5 Hindi words, spoken just before
the DASHX end card) and four scene lines: `- template | who_a | who_b | board | line`.
Templates: face-off (left=a, right=b, board = one short English word), run-up (bowler=a, batter=b),
shot (batter=a, watcher=b), catch (Dadaji catches; b = first watcher). Spoken lines: Devanagari only, no digits.
