The "DASHX Poster" tool is a private tool used only by the owner of DASHX to publish DASHX's own videos and posts to the owner's own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts.

The tool does not collect, store or share personal data of any visitor or customer. It only uses the owner's authorisation to upload the owner's own content.

Google user data

The tool requests access to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, copy or share any other Google user data.
